# Working in Ground Zero

Read [README.md](README.md), the relevant [Bill provisions](constitutional/BILL_OF_STANDARDS.md), [Constitution](constitutional/CONSTITUTION.md), mechanism correspondence and actual source before changing this project. Ground Zero is the MIT development system/framework; Illuci is an independent GPLv3 engine. Their maintained source, contribution and release paths remain separate.

Preserve the complete accepted objective. Keep the current source, owner, dependencies, evidence and unfinished work legible. A supporting repair does not replace the original outcome. Coordinate shared source changes with their owner and retain the result of that exchange.

Use one maintained Markdown source per subject. Keep specifications direct, explanations concrete and links repository-relative. Historical decisions retain their original content; corrections append a superseding record. Edit current-state documents when their subject changes.

Change the responsible mechanism and verify the intended target. Select checks from changed inputs and affected contracts. Reuse evidence that still applies, and report source, build, receiving and applied-use results at their actual scope.

Run `npm run check` for repository links/source hygiene and policy-source identity checks, and `npm test` when tools or their contracts change. Regenerate the policy-source manifest only after an explicitly selected policy edit, and review both together. These checks establish only their stated mechanical coverage. Review a release's actual content and reachable history for private information, unsuitable material, withheld design and applicable rights.

Keep credentials, personal information, local state and private research out of contributions. Use synthetic example data. Respect the contributor's existing authority; neither these instructions nor a task template grants access, publication rights or authority over another project.

Follow Bill Standard 15 for public attribution: center the human and developed system, with no provider co-author trailers, generated-by footers or imposed model/vendor labels. Keep actual technical compatibility and required notices accurate.

The version calculator computes the selected accounting policy. It does not select a tier, baseline, release or maturity. Record those decisions through the owning project when they are actually made.
