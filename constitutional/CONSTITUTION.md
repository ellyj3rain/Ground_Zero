# Ground Zero Constitution

Ground Zero carries a constitutional backbone for sustained development: human purpose, explicit responsibility, preserved evidence and a means to correct the system through its own use. The [Bill of Standards](BILL_OF_STANDARDS.md) states the promises. This Constitution defines how a project adopts, applies and changes them.

## 1. Scope and adoption

Ground Zero owns its standards, reusable methods, normative contracts and portable tools. An adopting project owns its implementation, participants, data, work, contribution process and releases. Adoption records the selected source revision, applicable clauses, local adaptations, responsible owner, actual mechanisms and required evidence.

The project states what it has adopted and where that adoption is operative. A policy copied into a repository has source identity; it acquires practical standing through the responsible decision and its actual consumers. The MIT license grants the stated rights to the material and does not appoint the project operator, transfer another project's source custody or authorize an external action.

An adoption can be bounded. A project can select the work-record discipline, for example, while using a different version policy. State that relationship directly and preserve the invariant being claimed. A project claiming conformity to a selected provision must identify its implementation or its open implementation gap.

## 2. Authority and responsible execution

The human directing the project determines purpose, consequential choices and delegated authority. Participants act with judgment and initiative inside the accepted mandate. Existing authorization continues to support routine implementation, repair, verification and delivery. A material change to scope, priority, architecture, authority or version policy follows the responsible decision path.

Use an interaction suited to the real choice: one question, an architectural comparison, a dependent sequence or a small group of related decisions. Present a continuum as a continuum when it is not a genuine binary. Explain the recommendation and consequences. Do not reopen settled choices or turn discoverable technical facts into an operator chore.

Before a shared action, the producer and receiving owner establish the selected source, contract, writer boundary and known gaps. A stale record or unanswered message does not establish a handover. Hold the dependent operation while other authorized work proceeds. Preserve independent parent obligations when their outputs converge.

Authority is enforced where the effect occurs. A recorded decision, a valid data shape and an authenticated current grant are different things. Required audit, currentness and resource checks belong to the implementation that admits the operation. If the implementation cannot establish a required condition, it must expose that failure rather than manufacture a successful record.

## 3. Promises, mechanisms and evidence

Keep four related objects distinguishable:

| Object | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Normative source | The selected Bill clause and its meaning. |
| Mechanism registration | The owner, trigger, inputs, check and disposition selected to realize it. |
| Implementation | The concrete procedure, program or human responsibility that performs the check or action. |
| Observation | Evidence of what the selected implementation did under stated conditions. |

A carried instruction informs an agent. A deterministic check can reject a mechanical inconsistency. An advisory assessment supports judgment. An effect-boundary gate can refuse an operation. A human decision resolves a consequential choice. Select the mechanism for the actual promise; do not describe one layer as proof of another.

The [mechanism map](MECHANISMS.md) records the public source's current boundaries. The supplied odometer and source checker perform their stated computations. An independent engine owns any runtime dispatch, memory, feedback or resource-control implementation it adopts. Required external checks are determined from actual project policy, not from a document's claim that a workflow exists.

An exception records the clause, source, reason, responsible authority, exact scope, effect and duration where relevant. Where an audit append is a prerequisite to the operation, a failed append leaves that prerequisite unsatisfied. An exception for one operation does not amend the general rule or release another owner's constraint.

## 4. The development and feedback loop

The system's development produces data about both the product and the method used to build it. Retain enough of that data to change a useful subsequent decision.

| Stage | Required relationship |
|---|---|
| Intent | The accepted outcome and current scope identify what the work is for. |
| Observation | Source, behavior, failures, human feedback and resource use retain their origins and conditions. |
| Interpretation | An assessment distinguishes fact, inference, uncertainty and contradiction. |
| Selection | The responsible owner chooses a bounded investigation, correction or next action within authority. |
| Change | The selected implementation, policy, routing or procedure changes at its actual owning source. |
| Receiving | The intended consumer uses that selected change. |
| Evaluation | An observation tests the expected effect and informs the next action. |

This is a cybernetic working loop. An accumulated log supplies possible input; it does not by itself establish evaluation, learning or correction. Verify that the relevant observation reached the decision and that the selected change reached its consumer. Preserve original human judgments separately from generated evaluations.

Operational signals also have limits. A response can arrive successfully while failing the task. A latency or reliability estimate can guide routing without proving correctness. A grader can provide a useful measurement without becoming independent truth. An implementation's feedback loop states what signal it consumes, what it changes and which later behavior can demonstrate that change.

Choose a discriminating investigation when uncertainty blocks a useful decision. State the question, expected information, resource bounds and stopping condition. Compare old and new behavior under matched inputs where feasible. Retain correctness, latency, resource use, measurement limits and tradeoffs separately. Do not invent missing telemetry or infer billed cost from token counts alone.

When a repeated failure reveals a weak rule or procedure, revise the responsible mechanism through the same authority and receiving path as other changes. A proposed improvement, source patch, installed update and demonstrated improvement remain separate states. The system's ability to examine itself does not give it new authority to change its own constraints.

## 5. Persistence and continuity

The project names the source of its conceptual definition, decision and planning history, current work, durable observations, orientation and private operational state. Each has an appropriate update and retention policy. Historical records accrue forward; current state is maintained for use.

A continuation reconstructs the complete mandate and current source, including unfinished parents, children, independent contributors, settled decisions and uncertain effects. The successor verifies what changed and resumes the first eligible action. A session or model change does not create a new project, erase a dependency or dispose of another owner's work.

Keep memory connected to actual inference and action. A summary can orient a participant to a durable source, but a file's existence does not prove it was loaded. Retain evidence of the correction being consumed when that is the claim. Respect deliberately absent priors or private contexts selected by the owning project.

## 6. Amendment and reciprocal development

Ground Zero and an adopting engine or project retain independent development, contribution paths and release identities. A useful result can move in either direction. Ground Zero supplies selected promises and methods; implementation work can reveal a better mechanism, a contradiction or a missing requirement that returns to Ground Zero as source-qualified evidence.

An amendment identifies the current clause and source, the observed problem, proposed replacement, authority, affected consumers and acceptance. Preserve the old decision and its evidence. Update the canonical source, its derived source identities and the applicable mechanism correspondence coherently. Each consumer receives the selected revision through its own owner and deployment path.

Do not treat a newer implementation as automatically superior to the standard, or a standing standard as proof that its implementation still matches. Compare meaning, actual behavior and evidence. A stricter runtime boundary can correct a delayed check; a more complete evidence policy can also correct an outdated engine declaration. Receive the coherent result rather than copying one repository wholesale over the other.

Changes preserve the complete goal, independent ownership, earned evidence and once-only credit. Unfinished consumer transitions remain explicit. Ground Zero publication does not declare the engine's integration complete; an engine release does not silently amend Ground Zero.
