# Origin and publication scope

Ground Zero refines the original Ground Zero Development System (GZDS), which grew through sustained development across projects, contributors and sessions. This public project is a curated and refined edition of its established working methods, source patterns and reusable tools.

The original development history remains privately preserved. The public tree carries the material selected for independent explanation, use and contribution. It does not import the original Git ancestry, private operational records or later undisclosed project designs.

The constitutional core reconciles the originating standards with developed implementation experience. Its Constitution and Bill remain the canonical policy source; the guides explain their use. The mechanism map identifies actual portable tools, independent engine responsibilities and the limits of observed evidence. This keeps the best established contributions of each project without merging their maintained source or releases.

The templates use neutral placeholders. The worked example is synthetic. The historical lessons preserve practical failures and corrections while omitting private identities and operational details. The version calculator implements the disclosed four-coordinate policy; its tests cover mechanical behavior rather than selecting a release baseline. The policy-source manifest pins the constitutional source and its provisions; it supplies identity for explicit adoption and no runtime authority of its own.

The original user-owned material and this public refinement are offered under the [MIT License](LICENSE). No third-party implementation, dataset or archived product is included by this source selection. A reference to another project or standard does not change its licensing terms.

Future contributions should retain the same distinction between established public methods, accurately attributed evidence and material that has not been cleared for publication.
